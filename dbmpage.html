About the Program

What is the Diamond Business Mastermind?

The Diamond Business Mastermind Program helps you as a jewelry entrepreneur or professional to optimize your sourcing, scale your business or career and automate your jewelry business in a systematic manner and a cost-efficient manner.

Who is the Diamond Business Mastermind Program for?

This program is designed for gold jewelry business owners, jewelry startups, scaling retailers, export businesses, and individuals looking to start a jewelry business or side hustle in diamonds.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before applying.

What is the cost of the Diamond Business Mastermind Program?
The cost is upwards of INR 1 Lakh depending on your need and structure of the DBM customised program. All other details are mentioned in the DBM E-Catalogue / Brochure.
Can beginners start a jewelry or diamond business?
Yes, the program is designed for beginners, startups, and existing business owners.
Is this program suitable for gold jewellers?
Yes, especially for those looking to shift to higher-margin diamond jewelry.
Do I need prior experience in diamonds?
No prior experience is required.
Is this available online or offline?
The program is delivered in a hybrid format (Online and on campus) with personalised consulting.
